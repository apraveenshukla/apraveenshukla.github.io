    AdaptFM Efficient LLM Challenge: Sixth Place with a 5.366× Speedup

    less than 1 minute read

    Published: August 23, 2026

    Together with Noel Thomas, I competed as team AFM-qd39x2e6 in the AdaptFM Efficient LLM Challenge at the ICML 2026 AdaptFM workshop. We finished sixth, achieving an official 5.366× mean speedup for Qwen3.5-4B inference on a single NVIDIA A10G while passing all three quality checks.

    Team credit

    This result was achieved jointly by Praveen A. Shukla and Noel Thomas at MBZUAI. The upcoming full write-up will present the work as our shared team effort.

    Key results

    • 64-token prompt: 345 ms (7.48× speedup)
    • 2,048-token prompt: 881 ms (6.18× speedup)
    • 8,192-token prompt: 2,684 ms (2.45× speedup)
    • Quality: MMLU-Pro 0.661, IFEval 0.845, and GPQA-Diamond 0.667

    The valid ranked configuration used a 4-bit AWQ Qwen3.5-4B target through AWQ-Marlin, a public DFlash drafter at K=15, and vLLM 0.20.1 with one mixed-dtype compatibility patch. It required no new training.

    The full blog will cover the experiments, failed paths, latency-versus-quality trade-offs, final architecture, and reproducibility lessons.
