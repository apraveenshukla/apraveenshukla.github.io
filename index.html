    Praveen Shukla

    Hi, I’m Praveen Shukla, an undergraduate AI researcher at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi. My research interests lie at the intersection of interpretability, world models, optimization, and AI safety.

    I’m currently studying how representations evolve inside neural networks, how models build and update internal world models, and how training and inference can be made more efficient and reliable.

    My work spans JEPA-style latent world models for transformers, personal world models for vision-language models, causal intervention and evaluation, and optimization of large language models.

    I am always open to collaboration!

    What I’m working on

    • Interpretability & Model Internals — studying representations, latent dynamics, causal interventions, and how changes inside transformers affect model behaviour.
    • World Models — developing latent and personal world models for understanding temporal dynamics, memory, preferences, and changing environments.
    • Optimization — exploring training dynamics, optimizers, learning efficiency, inference optimization, quantization, and efficient model execution.
    • AI Safety & Evaluation — building rigorous evaluations around model behaviour, intervention effects, robustness, reproducibility, and failure modes.
